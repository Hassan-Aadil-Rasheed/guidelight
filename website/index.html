Built around listening

Useful information, without visual clutter.

Voice first

Control the walk without hunting for buttons.

Speak destinations, choose places, repeat directions, scan surroundings, show the map or end the walk. The optional GuideLight button can operate Wispr Flow with two short presses.

“Scan surroundings”“Repeat directions”“Show map”“End walk”

Camera awareness

Spoken position, not false precision.

Alerts describe objects as left, ahead or right and high, middle or low in the image. GuideLight does not claim real-world distance.

Sound + haptics

Warnings you can hear and feel.

Rate-limited spoken alerts and distinct vibration patterns reduce repetition while keeping urgent camera cautions noticeable.

Know the limits

Experimental support, clearly described.

GuideLight is an early prototype. It has not been safety validated and must only supplement established mobility tools and training.

01

No depth measurement

Camera alerts use position and apparent image size. They cannot tell the real distance to an object.

02

Generic object detection

The current model can miss curbs, potholes, branches, vehicles and other important hazards.

03

No crossing decisions

GuideLight does not interpret traffic lights or decide when roads and crossings are safe.

04

Foreground and online

Search, maps, routing and Wispr Flow need internet. Scanning runs only while GuideLight is visible.

♿

Always continue using a cane or guide dog.
Use GuideLight only as a secondary source of information during supervised testing.
